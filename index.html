Background

Domain judgment behind the systems.

My path into AI is non-traditional. I spent years in investigations, insurance claims, regulated operations, research, and finance/property workflows — work that depends on incomplete information, evidence, judgment, documentation, and human interaction.

That is why I care about provenance, edge cases, cost controls, review gates, and whether a system can explain what it did — not simply whether a model generated an answer.
